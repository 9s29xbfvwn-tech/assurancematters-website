What we do

01

Governance

We review existing governance policies, identify gaps, and draft clear policies, terms of reference and reporting structures. Where it helps, we run briefing workshops so good practice becomes part of how teams work — not theory left on a shelf.

02

Assurance

Assurance for public- and private-sector projects. We provide the guidance and structures organisations need to give stakeholders assurance evidence relating to compliance — across security, safety and financial matters — and we help evaluate challenging or failing projects.

03

Metrics

Benchmarking, metrics and KPIs drawn from continuous research across industries, so leadership teams can measure compliance, risk and performance against meaningful, objective indicators.

04

Estimation

Adaptable estimation models built on relevant sector benchmarks, giving a realistic picture of the cost, effort and schedule a project involves — across information security, software, construction, change management, biometrics and infrastructure.

How we work

We work on a simple principle: governance and assurance should support delivery, not get in its way.

  • Led by Dr Carol Buttle, drawing on her long-standing information assurance experience.
  • Practical over theoretical — clear policies and working controls, written in plain language.
  • Evidence-based — benchmarking, metrics and KPIs that support clear stakeholder reporting.
  • Briefings and workshops — practical guidance for teams putting governance into practice.
